## P1. How long are backups kept?

The system will keep **backups** of its records, so that nothing is lost if a server fails. A backup is a copy taken
at a moment in time. When an old entry is deleted after two years, the backups taken **before** that day still hold
a copy of it until those backups are themselves deleted.

How long should backups be kept?

- ☐ **35 days.** A deleted entry is gone from every backup about five weeks after it is deleted. *(Our suggestion:
  long enough to recover from most mistakes, short enough to respect your two-year rule.)*
- ☐ 90 days
- ☐ Other:
- ☐ Not sure — please advise

*Backups are set up in a later stage, when the system goes live; this answer tells that stage what to do.*

## P3. Telling people their details are recorded

Staff, agents and business owners using AlphaKolect will have their internet address and device details recorded, and
a business owner can see those of their own staff and agents. Data protection law generally expects people to be
told what is recorded about them, why, who can see it and for how long.
