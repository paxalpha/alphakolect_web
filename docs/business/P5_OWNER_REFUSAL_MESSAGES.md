# AlphaKolect: What People Are Told When Something Is Not Allowed

**For:** the owner of AlphaKolect
**Prepared:** 14 October 2026
**Why:** When someone tries to do something their access does not allow, AlphaKolect refuses and shows a short
message. Until now, a staff member was always told **"Only the business owner can do this."** That was true while
staff could hold no permissions. Since the last step, an owner can give staff permissions through their role, so for
seven permissions the message is now wrong: the owner *can* allow it. The independent reviewer asked that you decide
the wording. Nothing else changes — the refusal itself, and its entry in the business's activity record, stay exactly
as they are.

As before, **"Not sure" is a fine answer.**

## R1. A staff member lacks a permission the owner can give

These are the seven permissions an owner can give: see the business profile, change it, see branches, manage
branches, see roles, see members, see invitations. A staff member whose role does not include the one needed is told:

- ☐ **"You don't have permission to do this. Ask the business owner if you need it."** *(Our suggestion: accurate,
  and tells them what to do.)*
- ☐ "Your role does not allow this."
- ☐ Other:
- ☐ Not sure

> Answer: You don't have permission to do this. Ask the business owner if you need it.

## R2. Something only the business owner can ever do

Managing roles, managing members, managing invitations and seeing the activity record can never be given to staff
(your earlier decisions). For these the message stays **"Only the business owner can do this."**

- ☐ **Keep it** *(Our suggestion: it is exactly true.)*
- ☐ Change to:

> Answer: Keep it

## R3. An AlphaKolect team member lacks a permission the super admin can give

From the next step, the super admin can give AlphaKolect team members (support, verification, finance) two
permissions: seeing the list of businesses and their members, and seeing the platform's activity record. Today such a
team member would be told "Only a platform super admin can do this", which will then be wrong in the same way. They
are told:

- ☐ **"You don't have permission to do this. Ask a super admin if you need it."** *(Our suggestion.)*
- ☐ Other:
- ☐ Not sure

> Answer: You don't have permission to do this. Ask a super admin if you need it.

## R4. Something only the super admin can ever do

Switching a business off or on, transferring a business to a new owner, and managing AlphaKolect team members are
the super admin's alone. Their messages stay as they are: **"Only a platform super admin may change a merchant's
status"**, **"Only a platform super admin may transfer ownership"** and **"Only a platform super admin can do this."**

- ☐ **Keep them** *(Our suggestion: they are exactly true.)*
- ☐ Change to:

> Answer: keep them
