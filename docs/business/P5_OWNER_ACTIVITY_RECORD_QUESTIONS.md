# AlphaKolect: Looking Through the Activity Record

**For:** the owner of AlphaKolect
**Prepared:** 17 October 2026
**Why:** Every business on AlphaKolect has an **activity record**: who did what, and when — including attempts that were
refused. You decided earlier that each business owner sees their own business's record and can look up one person's
activity in it, and that the AlphaKolect team's record is seen by the super admin and by team members given that
permission. Before the screens are built, a few things need deciding about **how much can be looked at in one go**.

We tested this on a large made-up record — two million entries over two years, from three thousand businesses — and
with the right set-up every choice below answers in well under a second. So these questions are about what makes sense
for the people using it, not about speed. Each page someone looks at is itself written in the record.

As before, **"Not sure" is a fine answer.**

## Q2. How long a period can one search cover?

Each search covers a period the person chooses (for example 1–31 March). To look further back, they search the next
period. Entries are kept for two years, as you decided. Earlier you said: *"One month at a time is fine. If needed, we
can implement a feature to allow searching longer periods in the future."*

- ☐ **Up to 31 days (one month) at a time** *(Our suggestion, and your earlier preference.)*
