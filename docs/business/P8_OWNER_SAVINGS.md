  suggestion: it covers the fixed and flexible packages businesses use today, e.g. "Alpha 1K", ₦1,000 a day.)*

## Q5. When the agent's cash does not match (a "remittance")

An agent hands over the day's cash; the business checks it against what the agent recorded.

- ☐ **The business approves the collections the cash covers; any it does not approve stay "recorded, awaiting the
  cash" until a later remittance covers them or the business cancels them (Q6). Every remittance and every
  collection left out is recorded against the agent** *(Our suggestion: nothing is lost, and a shortfall is visible.)*
- ☐ A remittance is all or nothing: approved whole, or rejected whole
