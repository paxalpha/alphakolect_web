more than intended — the switch **pauses every permission given to staff**, in every business at once (and,
separately, every permission given to AlphaKolect team members). **Business owners and AlphaKolect's super admin keep full
access**, so every business keeps running. Each use is recorded, and the switch is lifted once the fault is fixed and checked.

## Part C. The privacy rule (from the 12 October form, unchanged)

You decided that internet addresses and device details are kept for two years and then deleted, and that only business
owners (for their own business) and you may see them. Writing that into the system raised three practical questions.
They complete the written privacy rule, which comes back to you for approval before this stage ends.

### P1. How long are backups kept?

The system will keep **backups** of its records, so that nothing is lost if a server fails. A backup is a copy taken at
a moment in time. When an old entry is deleted after two years, the backups taken **before** that day still hold a copy
of it until those backups are themselves deleted.

- ☐ **35 days.** A deleted entry is gone from every backup about five weeks after it is deleted. *(Our suggestion:
  long enough to recover from most mistakes, short enough to respect your two-year rule.)*
- ☐ 90 days
- ☐ Other:
- ☐ Not sure — please advise

> Answer: 35 days

*Backups are set up in a later stage, when the system goes live; this answer tells that stage what to do.*
