## Part A: Staff inside a business

### A1. Staff who work at one branch

A business owner can tie a staff member to **one branch** (for example, Ikeja only). Some staff may also be allowed to
**add or invite other staff**.

When a staff member tied to one branch adds or invites someone, which branches may they give that person?

- ☐ **Only their own branch.** They can't give anyone access to other branches or to all branches. *(Our suggestion.)*
- ☐ **Any branch**, including all branches.
- ☐ Other:

> Answer: All staffs must be created by the business owner. Staffs can only be assigned to the branch they are created for. A staff cannot invite any other staff to the business. The business owner can assign a staff to any branch and can change the branch of a staff member at any time.

*Why it matters: otherwise a staff member limited to Ikeja could invite someone with access to every branch. That
doesn't matter yet, but it will once customers and collections are kept per branch.*

### A2. The business's activity record

Each business will have an **activity record**: who added a staff member, who changed a role, who changed the
business details, and attempts that were refused. It never shows passwords or codes, and never shows anything from
another business.

**A2a.** If a business owner allows a staff member to see this record, what should that staff member see?

- ☐ **The same as the owner.**
- ☐ **Less than the owner.** Please say what to hide:
- ☐ **Only the owner** may ever see it.

> Answer: Only the business owner may see the activity record. Staffs cannot see the activity record of the business.

*If staff are to see less than the owner, we'll make that a separate permission the owner can give, so it's always
clear who sees what. (Our suggestion.)*

**A2b.** Should the record show **refused attempts**? For example, "Tunde tried to change a role but wasn't allowed."

- ☐ Yes, to anyone who can see the record
- ☐ Only to the business owner
- ☐ No

> Answer: Yes, refused attempts should be shown to the business owner only. Staffs cannot see refused attempts.

**A2c.** Should it be possible to **see one person's actions only** (for example, everything Tunde did)? The old
system had this for your own staff.

- ☐ Yes *(our suggestion)*
- ☐ No

> Answer: Yes, the business owner should be able to see one person's actions only. Staffs cannot see one person's actions.

## Part B: Your own platform team (support, verification, finance)

### B1. Adding a team member whose email is already in use

Suppose you add someone to your platform team, but their email address already has a **business account** on
AlphaKolect (for example, they also run a savings business).

- ☐ **Refuse.** Ask them for a different email address, so their platform account is completely separate from any
  business account. *(Our suggestion.)*
- ☐ **Allow it.** One account can be both a business account and a platform team account.
- ☐ Other:

> Answer: Refuse. Ask them for a different email address, so their platform account is completely separate from any business account. This is to ensure that the platform team account and business account are distinct and do not interfere with each other.

### B2. Someone who leaves and comes back

When a team member leaves, we switch their account off and **remove all their permissions**. If they come back later:

- ☐ **Switch the same account back on, with no permissions.** You then give them only what they need again. *(Our
  suggestion.)*
- ☐ They must come back with a **new email address**.
- ☐ Switch it back on **with their old permissions**. *(Not suggested: they could get back access you forgot they had.)*

> Answer: Switch the same account back on, with no permissions. You then give them only what they need again.

### B3. Searching your platform activity record

You'll be able to search everything that happened across AlphaKolect, by person, business or type of action. We plan
to let you search **up to one month at a time**, 100 entries per page. We'll confirm these numbers after a speed test
with realistic data, so this is only to hear your preference.

- ☐ One month at a time is fine
- ☐ I'd need to search longer periods at once. How long?

> Answer: One month at a time is fine. If needed, we can implement a feature to allow searching longer periods in the future.

## Part D: Privacy — internet addresses and device details

Every recorded action also stores the **internet address** (IP address) and the **type of browser or phone** used.
This helps to investigate break-ins and disputes, but it is personal information, so we need a written rule before
this stage is finished. Data protection law generally expects personal information to be kept **no longer than
needed**. If you have a legal adviser, it's worth showing them your answers to this part.

**D1.** What do we keep this information **for**? (Tick all that apply.)

- ☐ Investigating break-ins and suspicious activity
- ☐ Settling disputes ("I never did that")
- ☐ Other:

> Answer: Investigate break-ins and suspicious activity, and settling disputes. This information is crucial for maintaining the security and integrity of the platform.

**D2.** Who may **see** it?

- ☐ Only you
- ☐ You, and platform team members you give permission to
- ☐ Also each business owner, for their own business

> Answer: Only the business owner may see this information. Staffs cannot see the IP addresses or device details of other users.

**D3.** How **long** do we keep it?

- ☐ 90 days
- ☐ 1 year
- ☐ 2 years
- ☐ Other:
- ☐ Not sure — please propose something

> Answer: 1 year. This allows sufficient time for investigation and dispute resolution while minimizing the retention of personal information.

**D4.** After that time:

- ☐ **Remove only the address and device details**, and keep the rest of the record *(our suggestion)*
- ☐ Delete the whole entry

> Answer: Delete the whole entry. This ensures that personal information is not retained longer than necessary, in compliance with data protection principles.

**D5.** Should the rule also apply to records **already stored**?

- ☐ Yes
- ☐ No, only from now on

> Answer: Yes, the rule should apply to records already stored to ensure compliance with data protection principles.
