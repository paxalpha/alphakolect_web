## What this stage built

- **Who may do what.** Every action in the system now checks a written list of permissions. You decide what your staff
  may do, role by role; staff cannot give themselves more. The AlphaKolect team members have only the permissions the
  super admin gives them, person by person.
- **Checked twice.** Each permission is checked when a request arrives and again by the database at the moment of the
  change, so a permission you remove stops working straight away.
- **The activity record.** Sign-ins, refused attempts and every change to permissions or business settings are
  recorded. You can read your business's record (who did what, when, from which address and device). Actions by the
  AlphaKolect team show as "AlphaKolect team", as you decided.
- **Your privacy decisions.** Activity entries are kept two years, then deleted in full, the clean-up run once a month
  (your decision). You may ask for a copy of the entries before they are deleted. It is handed to you in person on a
  stick, and nothing it covers is deleted until you confirm in writing that you have it. Sign-in and link details are
  cleared on the same two-year rule. The written policy you signed is in force.
- **An emergency switch.** If a permission is ever given too widely, staff permissions can be paused at once while
  every business owner and the super admin keep full access, as you decided.

## What remains before closure and what is left for later

- **The first copy hand-over is rehearsed before it is used.** The parts that need a real USB stick and real disks
  could not be tested automatically. Before the first real copy reaches you, the operator rehearses them once on test
  data, following a written checklist, and records the result.
- **Independent review is still required.** The reviewer must complete and record the P5.8, P5.9 and P5.10 gate
  reviews before you are asked to approve closing this stage.
- **Scheduled clean-up and alerts** come in later stages; until then the clean-up is run by hand, once a month.
