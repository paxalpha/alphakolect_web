## F1. Who manages staff inside a business

You said: *"All staff must be created by the business owner. Staff can only be assigned to the branch they are
created for. A staff cannot invite any other staff."*

Earlier (11 October) you had decided that staff **could** be given permission to invite or manage other staff, but
only up to their own level. Your new answer replaces that, so we want to be sure of its reach.

**F1a.** Which of these should **only the business owner** be able to do? (Tick all that apply.)

- ☐ Invite new staff or agents, and cancel invitations - Yes, only the owner can do this
- ☐ Change a staff member's role or branch - Yes, only the owner can do this
- ☐ Switch a staff member or agent off, or back on - Yes, only the owner can do this

> Answer: All 3. 

**F1b.** Can a staff member ever have access to **all branches** (today's default when no branch is chosen), or must
every staff member belong to **exactly one branch**?

- ☐ Every staff member belongs to exactly one branch
- ☐ The owner may choose one branch or all branches
- ☐ Other:

> Answer: The owner may choose one branch or all branches.

*Why it matters: if every staff member must belong to one branch, existing staff without a branch will need one, and
the owner must always pick a branch when inviting staff.*

- ☐ **Each business owner**, for their own business only, **and** me
- ☐ **Each business owner only**, for their own business. Not even the AlphaKolect team.

*Why it matters: if business owners can see them, the address and device of their staff appear in their activity
record. If only you can, they appear only in the AlphaKolect-wide record.*

## F4. Deleting whole entries after one year

You chose to **delete the whole entry** after one year. That keeps no personal information longer than a year, which
is a good principle. It also means the system forgets, after one year:

- who changed a staff member's role or permissions,
- who switched a business off or moved its ownership,
- who added or removed your own platform team members.

These records often matter in a dispute or an investigation that starts later, or when an auditor or regulator asks.
The other option keeps the "who did what, when" and removes only the internet address and device details after one
year.

- ☐ **Delete the whole entry** after one year, as I said
- ☐ **Remove only the address and device details** after one year, and keep the rest
- ☐ **Keep the rest longer, then delete it.** For how long?
- ☐ I'd like to ask my legal adviser first

> Answer: Delete the whole entry after two years. The owner may ask for a record of the deleted entries, but the system will not keep them.

*Why it matters: today the activity record cannot be changed or deleted by anyone, by design. Any deletion becomes one
controlled, recorded clean-up job, built in this stage. A full deletion also cannot be undone.*
