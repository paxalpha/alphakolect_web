**Why:** In the next stage, a business owner will be able to give their staff permissions through roles, for example
a "Cashier" role that may see the list of branches. The screen where the owner chooses a role's permissions shows a
one-line description of each permission. Business owners will rely on these words when deciding what their staff may
do, so the words must be clear and accurate. Below is our suggested wording.

The description only **explains** a permission. It does not change what the permission allows: the system enforces
each permission whatever its description says. The list of permissions, and which ones only the owner can hold, were
settled by your earlier answers and are not being asked again.

For each line, tick **Agree** or write the wording you prefer. As before, **"Not sure" is a fine answer.**
