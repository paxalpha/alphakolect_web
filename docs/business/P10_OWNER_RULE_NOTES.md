## What you decided on 11 November 2026

- **The trial** starts on the day the business confirms its email, and lasts 90 days. A sign-up that never confirms
  isn't a real business (rule 2.2), and a business that confirms late doesn't lose trial days. *(Q1)*
- **An agent is active** if, in the 15 days before the billing date, the agent signed in to the mobile app or recorded a
  collection. *(Q2)*

## What this does not change

- Every other rule stays exactly as it is.
- Nothing is charged before you switch billing on (rule 1.3).
