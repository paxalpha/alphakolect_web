# AlphaKolect: Subscriptions and Billing

**For:** the owner of AlphaKolect
**Prepared:** 11 November 2026
**Why:** The next stage builds AlphaKolect's own billing: the monthly subscription each business pays from its Live
wallet into the Platform Live wallet, what happens when it can't pay, and how access comes back. Your signed rules
already settle most of it:

- the prices by number of agents (rule 10.1);
- prices edited in the platform admin (10.2);
- the subscription taken from the business's Live wallet (10.3);
- suspension and what each person sees (10.4);
- access returning automatically once paid (10.5);
- agents counted only if active in the last 15 days (10.6a).

**Billing stays paused (rule 1.3)** until you switch it on, and it can't be switched on before TransactPay confirms how
money moves from a business's wallet to AlphaKolect's. The questions below are what your rules leave open. Each has our
suggestion.

As before, **"Not sure" is a fine answer**.

## The permissions' wording

These are the descriptions the platform admin and the business owner would see. Mark any you want reworded.

| Permission | Who | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `merchant.subscription.read` | business | See the subscription: price, trial, billing date, invoices and the amount owed |
| `merchant.subscription.pay` | business | Ask AlphaKolect to take the amount owed from the Live wallet now |
| `platform.billing.read` | platform staff | See every business's billing: state, invoices, amount owed, and what billing would charge |
| `platform.billing.prices` | platform staff | Set new prices and the date they start (subject to Q8) |
| `platform.billing.custom_price` | platform staff | Set the price of a business with 21 or more agents |
| `platform.billing.switch` | Super Admin only | Switch billing on or off; mark a business as not billed |
