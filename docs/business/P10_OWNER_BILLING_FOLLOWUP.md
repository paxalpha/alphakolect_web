## F1. Unpaid months when a business is marked "not billed"

You may mark a business as not billed (Q11). Suppose it already owes one or more months when you do.

- ☐ **What it owes is cancelled. Each cancelled month is kept in its records as "waived", with your reason, and the
  business regains access at once if it was suspended** *(Our suggestion: "not billed" means not billed, and the record
  shows what was let go and why.)*
- ☐ It still owes them; it just isn't billed for new months

> Answer: what is owed is cancelled. Each cancelled month is kept in its records as "waived", with your reason, and the business regains access at once if it was suspended

  finished and recorded, because it can't be called back** *(Our suggestion: rule 1.3 as written, without losing what
  is owed.)*
