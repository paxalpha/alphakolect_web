## Part A: Staff inside a business

### A1. Staff who work at one branch

A business owner can tie a staff member to **one branch** (for example, Ikeja only). Some staff may also be allowed to
**add or invite other staff**.

When a staff member tied to one branch adds or invites someone, which branches may they give that person?

- ☐ **Only their own branch.** They can't give anyone access to other branches or to all branches. *(Our suggestion.)*
- ☐ **Any branch**, including all branches.
- ☐ Other:

> Answer:

*Why it matters: otherwise a staff member limited to Ikeja could invite someone with access to every branch. That
doesn't matter yet, but it will once customers and collections are kept per branch.*

Each business will have an **activity record**: who added a staff member, who changed a role, who changed the
business details, and attempts that were refused. It never shows passwords or codes, and never shows anything from
another business.

### B2. Someone who leaves and comes back

When a team member leaves, we switch their account off and **remove all their permissions**. If they come back later:

- ☐ **Switch the same account back on, with no permissions.** You then give them only what they need again. *(Our
  suggestion.)*
- ☐ They must come back with a **new email address**.
- ☐ Switch it back on **with their old permissions**. *(Not suggested: they could get back access you forgot they had.)*

needed**. If you have a legal adviser, it's worth showing them your answers to this part.
