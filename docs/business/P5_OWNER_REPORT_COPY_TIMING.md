# AlphaKolect: When Old Entries Are Deleted, If You Asked for a Copy

**For:** the owner of AlphaKolect
**Prepared:** 18 October 2026
**Why:** You decided that every activity entry is kept for two years and then deleted, and that you may ask for a copy
of the entries about to be deleted. You also decided how the copy reaches you: handed to you in person, and our copy
destroyed as soon as you confirm in writing that you have it.

One thing was never asked: **whether the old entries may be deleted before you have confirmed you have the copy.**
It matters because the copy is the only record of those entries once they are deleted.

## C1. When you have asked for a copy, when are the old entries deleted?

- ☐ **Only after you confirm you have the copy** *(Our suggestion.)* The copy is made and checked, handed to you, and
  the entries are deleted once your written "received" is recorded. Nothing can be lost: if the copy goes missing
  before it reaches you, we simply make a new one from the entries, which are still there. The cost: entries you asked
  a copy of stay a few days past their two years, until you confirm.
- ☐ **Straight away, once the copy is made and checked.** Entries are deleted on time. The risk: if the copy is then
  lost or damaged before it reaches you, it can only be rebuilt from a backup less than 35 days old — and backups are
  only set up later, before launch. After that, those entries are gone for good.
- ☐ Not sure

> Answer: only after I confirm you have the copy

## C2. Only if you chose "Straight away": if the copy is lost and cannot be rebuilt?

- ☐ **We tell you, record it, and your request counts as done.** Later clean-ups delete without a copy until you ask
  again.
- ☐ **Your request stays open.** Every later clean-up makes you a new copy of what it deletes, until one reaches you.
